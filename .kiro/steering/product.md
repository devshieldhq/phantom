# PHANTOM Product Steering
PHANTOM resurrects the spirit of Bulletin Board Systems as a modern, welcoming community network.
Tagline: "The internet never really dies."
Audience: curious builders, archivists, gamers, online communities, and people seeking meaningful digital spaces.
Experience: hybrid retro terminal language + modern accessible dashboard.
Principles: community ownership, durable knowledge, low-bandwidth resilience, privacy, graceful degradation, transparent AI.
MVP priorities: rooms, messaging, archive, auth, safety. Stretch: offline queue, AI summaries, text multiplayer game.
Never claim demo data is live user activity. Mark prototype/sample content clearly.
